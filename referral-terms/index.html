Referral terms.

Kwadrat Legacy Studio Limited · 1 September 2026 to 31 December 2026

  1. Who these terms are with

    The scheme is operated by Kwadrat Legacy Studio Limited, a company registered in England and Wales, number 16606965, registered office 34 Olinda Road, London N16 6TL (“the studio”). By making a post through the studio’s sharing page you accept these terms.

  2. Who may take part

    Anyone aged 18 or over may share the studio’s work. Taking part creates no employment, partnership or agency between you and the studio, and you may not hold yourself out as speaking for it, quote prices, or make any promise on its behalf.

  3. How a referral is recorded

    Your post carries the WhatsApp number you entered. An enquiry reaches the studio marked with it. That marking is the only record of a referral: the studio keeps no list, and an enquiry arriving without it cannot be credited to anyone.

    One person may reach the studio more than once, through the posts of different sharers. If they order within three months of their first enquiry, the commission belongs to the sharer who introduced them. If more than three months have passed since that first enquiry, it belongs to the sharer whose post most recently brought them back. Only one commission is paid for any one client.

  4. What earns a commission

    A commission arises when a person you referred commissions a lineage scroll (megillas yuchsin) or a yichus sefer. It is earned when their deposit reaches the studio.

  5. What the commission is

    US$1,000 for each client who orders, or £750 where payment is made in sterling — whatever they order and however many works they commission at once. Both figures are fixed, so neither the exchange rate on the day nor anything about what a family bought comes into it.

    There is no cap. Every client you bring who orders earns you the full amount.

  6. When it is paid

    A commission is paid once the client’s deposit has reached the studio, by bank transfer or another means agreed between you. The studio will contact you on the number your post carried.

    Before any payment is made you must complete the studio’s claim form. It is the studio’s record of the payment, and no commission is processed without it.

    Bank details are settled with you directly, never through the form.

  7. What the payment depends on

    Three things, and the studio cannot pay without all of them:

    That your post was made through the studio’s own sharing page. A message you wrote yourself, or a link you assembled by hand, cannot be credited.

    That the link in your post was left exactly as it was given to you. A shortened, retyped or edited link loses the marking that identifies you.

    That the enquiry reached the studio carrying that marking. If the person you referred deletes it before writing, or contacts the studio some other way, nothing connects them to you, and the studio cannot be held responsible for that.

  8. How long the scheme runs

    The scheme runs to 31 December 2026. A commission arises on any order placed on or before that date, whenever the deposit follows it. An order placed after that date earns nothing, so take your posts down when the scheme closes.

    A commission arising from an order placed in time is paid in full after the scheme has ended.

  9. Who does not count

    No commission is due on someone already in conversation with the studio, on a former or existing client returning for further work, or on yourself, your spouse, or anyone in your household. The studio’s own records determine whether a person was already known to it.

  10. How you may and may not share

    Share the studio’s post as it was given to you. Do not alter its wording or its link, send it to people who have not asked to hear from you, post it where it is unwelcome, or place paid advertising against the studio’s name. Do not present a family’s work, or any image of it, as your own.

    The studio may withhold a commission earned through sharing that breaches this clause, and may end your part in the scheme.

  11. Your number

    The number you enter is not stored by the studio. It travels inside the post you make and reaches the studio only when someone writes in through it. The studio uses it to know whom to pay and to reach you, and for nothing else.

  12. Tax

    A commission is paid gross. What you owe on it is a matter between you and HMRC, or your own tax authority, and the studio deducts nothing and accounts for nothing on your behalf.

  13. Changes and endings

    The scheme is intended to run to 31 December 2026. The studio may change these terms, or end it earlier, by posting a revised page here. A commission already earned when a change takes effect is paid under the terms that stood when it was earned.

  14. Disagreements

    Speak to the studio first — almost everything is settled that way. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
